Our Story

A Trusted Name in Nepal-China Trade

Nepal Shandong International Import and Export Pvt. Ltd. was founded to simplify cross-border trade between Nepal and China. Operating from our office in Bhaktapur, we work closely with manufacturers, producers, and buyers on both sides to keep goods moving reliably and transparently.

Our team manages every step of the process — sourcing, documentation, customs, and logistics — so our clients can focus on growing their business rather than navigating trade complexities.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, transparent, and efficient import and export services that strengthen trade ties between Nepal and China.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted import-export partner for businesses trading between Nepal and China.
