Who We Are

Connecting Markets, Building Trust

Nepal Shandong International Import and Export Pvt. Ltd. is a Nepal-based trading company built on a simple purpose: to make cross-border trade between Nepal and China simple, dependable, and profitable for our partners.

Drawing on strong relationships with suppliers and buyers across Shandong and other Chinese provinces, we help Nepali businesses source quality goods from China, and help Chinese buyers access authentic Nepali products — backed by careful sourcing, documentation, and logistics support at every step.
